What a tune-up covers

What we check

Heating

Burners, igniter and flame sensor, heat exchanger, safety controls, filter, and carbon monoxide at the unit. On boilers: pressure, expansion tank, circulators and radiator vents.

Cooling

Coils, capacitor and contactor, refrigerant pressures, condensate drain, and the temperature drop across the system.

Plumbing

Water pressure, shutoff valves, visible supply and drain lines, toilets, the sump pump, and the water heater's relief valve and anode.
