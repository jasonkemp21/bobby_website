Know what you're looking at

Signs it's time to call

Running but not cooling
Check the filter and the outdoor unit's breaker first. If both are fine, the cause is often a failed capacitor or low refrigerant from a leak.
Ice on the copper lines
Turn the system off and let it thaw. Ice means poor airflow or low refrigerant, and running it frozen can kill the compressor.
Water near the indoor unit
The condensate drain is clogged. It is a quick fix if caught early and a ceiling repair if not.
A system from before 2010
It probably runs on R-22, which has not been made in the US since 2020. Recharging one is expensive, so a leak is usually the point to replace.

No ducts? That is common here

Homes heated by radiators were built without ductwork. Ductless mini-splits cool them without tearing into plaster walls: one outdoor unit, a small line to each room, and each room on its own thermostat.

Common questions

Air conditioning questions

How often should AC be serviced?

Once a year, in spring. A tune-up covers the coils, the drain, the electrical parts and the refrigerant pressures.

How long does a central air system last?

Around twelve to fifteen years on average. Past that, efficiency drops and parts fail more often.

Can I add central air to a house with radiators?

Yes, but the ductwork is the hard part. Ductless mini-splits or a high-velocity small-duct system are usually the better fit in an older home.

Why is one floor always hotter?

Heat rises and most systems have one thermostat downstairs. Balancing dampers, a second zone or a ductless unit upstairs fixes it.
