Smell gas? Leave first.

Get everyone out of the building. Don't touch light switches and don't use a phone inside. From outside, call 911 or your gas utility: PECO 1-800-841-4141 in the suburbs, PGW 215-235-1212 in Philadelphia. Call us after the utility has made it safe, and we will repair the line.

While you wait

What to do right now

A few minutes of the right steps can save a ceiling, a floor or a furnace.

Burst or leaking pipe

  1. Close the main water shutoff. It is usually where the line enters the basement, by the meter.
  2. Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain what is left in the pipes.
  3. If water is near outlets or the panel, switch off power to that area at the breaker.
  4. Move what you can and start soaking up water.

Water heater leaking

  1. Close the cold water valve on the pipe above the heater.
  2. Gas unit: turn the dial on the gas valve to off. Electric unit: switch off its breaker.
  3. If the tank is emptying onto the floor, run a hose from the drain valve to a floor drain or outside.

No heat

  1. Check that the thermostat is set to heat and its batteries are good.
  2. Check the furnace or boiler switch and its breaker.
  3. Replace the filter if it is clogged.
  4. In freezing weather, open cabinet doors under sinks and let faucets drip so pipes do not freeze.

Sewer backing up

  1. Stop running water everywhere in the house. No flushing, no laundry, no dishwasher.
  2. Keep children and pets away from the water.
  3. Don't use chemical drain cleaner. It will not clear a main line and it makes the repair hazardous.

Frozen pipe

  1. Close the main shutoff in case the pipe has already split.
  2. Open the faucet the pipe feeds.
  3. Warm the pipe gently with a hair dryer, starting at the faucet end. Never use an open flame.

Carbon monoxide alarm

  1. Get everyone outside into fresh air.
  2. Call 911 from outside.
  3. Don't go back in or run the heat until the fire department clears the house. Then call us to find the cause.
