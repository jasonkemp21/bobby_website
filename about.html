About Kennedy & Smith Mechanical

A two-man, owner-operated plumbing and HVAC company based in Havertown, Pennsylvania.

The shop

Two owners, both on the tools

Kennedy & Smith Mechanical is Bob Smith and Joe Kennedy. Bob is a master plumber. Joe is a senior HVAC technician. They started the company in Havertown, and they still do the work themselves.

It is two people on purpose. A small shop can take fewer jobs and give each one the time it needs, and that was the reason for starting one.

When you call, you talk to an owner. The same person looks at the job, prices it and installs it. Nobody here is paid to sell you more than you need.

They work in the towns they live in, often for people they know, and they would like to keep it that way.
